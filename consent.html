EYE TRACKING TESTS AND RESULTS

RESEARCH STUDY CONSENT FORM

Utah Valley University | Participant Consent for Research

1. About the Study

You are invited to participate in a website usability research study. This research project is being conducted by Utah Valley University student researchers.

There are no costs for participating in the study. The information will help us understand how people conduct specific tasks. The data collected may not benefit you directly, but what is learned from this study should provide general benefits to UVU and researchers.

2. What to Expect

The test will consist of participants being calibrated to an Imotions eye tracker, which will emit a harmless, invisible infrared laser into the participant's eyes. Participants will then complete a pre-test survey to collect demographic information, the eye-tracking portion of the test, where the participant will view a series of stimuli, and a post-survey test. This study will be completed after the interviewer has finished and has no further questions or after a 15-25-minute window.

3. Your Data

Recordings of the eye-tracting and screencapture from the study will be stored on UVU computers and will be accessible to the researchers. The original recordings will not be shared with the client. All other data gathered from the biometric or survey portions of the test will be used by the UVU researchers for analysis and presented in a summarized format to the client.

Identifiers might be removed from the identifiable private information, and after such removal, the information could be used for future research studies or distributed to another investigator for future research studies without additional informed consent from you.

4. Voluntary Participation

Your participation in this study is voluntary. At any point, you have the option to withdraw from the study. If you have any questions or concerns about this study, contact Professor Dan Hatch at daniel.hatch@uvu.edu.
